Privacy policy

Post Sender does not have its own servers and does not collect, store or share your personal data.

  • Access tokens and account details you enter are stored only on your phone, in encrypted storage.
  • When you sign in with Google, the app uses that access only to upload videos to your own YouTube channel at your request.
  • Your posts are sent directly from your phone to the platforms you select, and nowhere else.
  • You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account settings, or by uninstalling the app.
